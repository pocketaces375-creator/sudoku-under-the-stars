Privacy Policy

Sudoku Under the Stars
Last updated: 16 September 2026

The short version

This app collects nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no network requests. It does not know who you are and never contacts a server.

What is stored

Your puzzle progress, best scores and sound preference are saved on your own device so the app can pick up where you left off. This data never leaves your device, is not readable by us, and is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

What is not collected

No name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage analytics, or crash reports. The app requests no permissions.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It complies with COPPA by collecting nothing at all.

Third parties

There are none. No SDKs, no ad networks, no analytics providers, no payment processors. Nothing in the app shares data with anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
