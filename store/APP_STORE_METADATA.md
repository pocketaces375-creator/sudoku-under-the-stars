## Description (4000 char max)
```
Eighty-eight constellations. Eighty-eight puzzles. One quiet evening at a time.

Sudoku Under the Stars is a sudoku game with something worth looking at. Every puzzle is paired with one of the 88 constellations astronomers use to map the sky, drawn from real star positions, and every one carries a short, true fact about it.

Finish a row, a column or a box and part of the constellation above your grid lights up. Complete the puzzle and the whole figure burns bright — then the grid collapses into a black hole, with a photon ring, an accretion disk, and the background stars bending around it.

HOW IT PLAYS
Numbers launch from the keypad and arc into the grid like a pinball table. Land them in a row and your combo climbs to five times score. Get one wrong and the board tilts.

WHAT IS INSIDE
• 88 levels, one per constellation, from Orion to Vulpecula
• Three difficulties that arrive gradually as you go
• Every puzzle verified to have exactly one solution — no guessing, ever
• Pencil marks, hints, and a full undo of any square
• A star map tracking every constellation you have solved
• True facts throughout, written from published astronomical references
• A few secrets hidden in the stars themselves

NO NONSENSE
No ads. No accounts. No subscriptions. No internet connection required. Nothing is collected about you, ever. Just the puzzles and the sky.

From The Lucid Mind Collection, makers of the large-print puzzle book of the same name.
```
